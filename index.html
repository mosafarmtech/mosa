About MOSA
We believe technology should work with farmers.

MOSA stands for Mobile Organic Smart Agriculture. We are a team of young innovators working on practical robotic solutions for agriculture.

Our goal is to develop technology that understands real farming conditions and supports farmers in their daily work.

Our Vision

To make agricultural technology accessible, practical and useful for farmers.

Our Mission

To develop smart robotic solutions for real-world farming challenges.
